E-commerce Redesign

Complete redesign of a fashion e-commerce platform to improve user experience and increase conversion rates.

Overview

The client, a leading fashion retailer, approached us to completely reimagine their online shopping experience. Their existing platform was outdated, difficult to navigate, and had a cart abandonment rate of over 75%. Our goal was to create a modern, intuitive shopping experience that would delight customers and drive sales.

Challenge

The main challenges we faced included a complex product catalog with over 10,000 SKUs, the need to support multiple languages and currencies, integration with legacy inventory systems, and maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints. We also needed to ensure the new design would work seamlessly across all devices.

Solution

We developed a clean, minimalist design that puts products front and center. The new navigation system uses AI-powered search and smart filters to help customers find exactly what they're looking for. We implemented a streamlined checkout process that reduced steps from 5 to 3, and added features like save-for-later and quick buy to reduce friction.
